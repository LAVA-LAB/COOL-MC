Wolf, Goat, and Cabbage river-crossing puzzle.

A farmer must move a wolf, a goat, and a cabbage from the LEFT bank
(position 0) to the RIGHT bank (position 1). The boat fits only the
farmer plus at most one of the wolf, the goat, or the cabbage per crossing.

Constraints (any violation ends the run as UNSAFE):
- If the wolf and the goat are on the same bank without the farmer,
  the wolf eats the goat.
- If the goat and the cabbage are on the same bank without the farmer,
  the goat eats the cabbage.

Goal: reach the state where farmer=1, wolf=1, goat=1, cabbage=1.

- farmer  = {farmer}    (0 = left, 1 = right)
- wolf    = {wolf}      (0 = left, 1 = right)
- goat    = {goat}      (0 = left, 1 = right)
- cabbage = {cabbage}   (0 = left, 1 = right)

Available actions:
- cross_alone    : the farmer crosses by itself.
- cross_wolf     : the farmer takes the wolf across (must be on the
                   same bank as the farmer).
- cross_goat     : the farmer takes the goat across (must be on the
                   same bank as the farmer).
- cross_cabbage  : the farmer takes the cabbage across (must be on the
                   same bank as the farmer).
